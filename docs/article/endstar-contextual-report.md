# Contextual Report

## Endstar: Platform Definition, Visual System, and UAE Context

**Prepared for:** UAE Character Design Research Project
**Endless Studios / E-Line Media.** Vinícius Vieira Cavalcanti
**Date:** May 2026

---

## Purpose of This Report

This report compiles publicly available information about Endstar, the game-making platform developed by Endless Studios and published in partnership with E-Line Media, for citation and contextual reference in the academic article "Between Identity and Stylization: Evidence-Based Criteria for Representing Emirati Characters in Stylized 3D Game Environments." All information contained herein was extracted from official Endless Studios digital publications and is cited accordingly.

The report is organized in four sections: (1) institutional definition of Endless Studios and its relationship with E-Line Media; (2) technical and aesthetic description of Endstar as a platform and visual system; (3) the platform's strategic connection to the UAE market; and (4) citation guidance for academic use.

---

## 1. Institutional Definition

### 1.1 Endless Studios

Endless Studios defines itself as "a community of game-makers and game industry mentors" operating under the belief that "by building and collaborating on games, anyone can learn marketable digital skills such as Digital Art, Design, Engineering, Management, and Go-to-Market" (Endless Studios, 2024a). The studio was originally founded with roots in educational gaming. Its team describes over ten years of impact through products including Minecraft.Edu and the National STEM Video Game Challenge, which together reached millions of learners (Endless Studios, 2024a).

The institution functions simultaneously as a game development studio, an educational platform, and a professional mentorship community. It operates a structured four-level learning pathway (Launchpad, Community Game Making, Capstone Projects, and Pro Studio) designed to take participants from first-time creators to junior professional developers (Endless Studios, 2024b). The program is positioned as accessible to young aspiring developers between the ages of 13 and 17, with no prior experience required.

### 1.2 Relationship with E-Line Media

Endless Studios and E-Line Media operate as sister studios within the same organizational structure. The relationship is formally defined on the Endless Studios website: the highest level of the learning pathway, designated Pro Studio, involves participants working "on professionally published games alongside our sister game studio, E-Line Media" (Endless Studios, 2024b). This partnership positions Endstar as both an educational tool and a commercially developed product, with character and world art developed by professional artists employed at E-Line Media.

The formal incorporation of Endless Studios into E-Line Media occurred in 2026. Prior to this, both organizations operated in close collaboration, with shared creative direction and production infrastructure. Vinícius Vieira Cavalcanti joined the team through Endless Studios in February 2022 and continued as Senior 3D Character Artist following the incorporation into E-Line Media.

## 2. Endstar: Platform and Visual System

### 2.1 Official Definition

Endstar is officially described by Endless Studios as "the easiest Game Engine for All Levels" and positioned with the following defining statement: "Build, code, and create together in real time — no experience required" (Endless Studios, 2024c). The platform is explicitly compared to Minecraft.edu as a reference experience for collaborative real-time creation, signaling its positioning in the educational game-making space while maintaining professional production values in its visual output.

### 2.2 Technical Architecture

Endstar is built on three technical pillars. The first is a **real-time 3D collaborative environment**, a persistent world in which multiple users can build, code, and play simultaneously without requiring individual setups or session breaks. The second is **LUA scripting**, a lightweight programming language embedded in the platform that allows users to customize object behaviors, game mechanics, and interactive elements without external development environments. The third is a **Unity-based SDK**, a software development kit that allows external creators, including professional artists at E-Line Media and Endless Studios, to develop custom assets, props, tilesets, and character models for integration into the platform (Endless Studios, 2024c).

As of the most recent documented update (version 0.3.7, December 2024), the platform remains in Alpha state. New content is added on a weekly basis, including props and character assets developed by the professional art team. The December 2024 update noted the introduction of the Date Palm as a new prop, a culturally specific asset signaling active development of UAE-contextual content within the platform's asset library (Endless Studios, 2024c).

### 2.3 The Endstar Visual System

The visual style of Endstar, designated internally as the Endstar visual system, represents a fully developed and codified aesthetic framework within which all character and environment art must cohere. The system is characterized by a stylized 3D aesthetic with strong, readable silhouettes, graphic clarity in the simplification of forms, and a consistent level of detail that differentiates Endstar characters from both photorealistic game productions and more heavily caricatured cartoon styles.

The approved character library visible in official Endstar materials includes a diverse roster of human and non-human characters, all developed by the professional art team at E-Line Media and Endless Studios. This roster constitutes the visual benchmark against which all new character designs, including the Emirati female characters discussed in the academic article, must be calibrated. A new character that does not cohere visually with this established roster, regardless of its individual quality, would fail the system coherence test.

The Endstar visual system therefore functions as the second pole of tension in the research problem of the academic article: authentic Emirati character representation must be achieved without breaking coherence with this established visual language. The platform's SDK and Unity foundation mean that character geometry, texture resolution, polygon count, and rigging must meet real-time game production standards, adding technical constraints to the aesthetic ones.

### 2.4 Character Development Pipeline

Character development for Endstar follows a professional game production pipeline involving concept development, high-resolution sculpting in ZBrush, retopology, UV mapping, texturing, rigging, and real-time implementation. The Senior 3D Character Artist at E-Line Media, the author's role, is responsible for this complete pipeline for assigned characters, including creative direction on cultural authenticity, visual development, and final implementation.

The UAE Female character, which constitutes the primary empirical object of the academic article, was developed within this pipeline under the creative direction of the studio's Creative Director. The character's design integrates traditional Emirati female dress (specifically the burqa facial mask, the Abuteela traditional dress, the shayla head covering, and traditional jewelry) within the Endstar visual system's level of stylization.

## 3. Endstar and the UAE Market

### 3.1 Institutional Strategic Alignment

Endless Studios maintains an explicitly documented strategic relationship with the UAE market. The official website contains a dedicated section for "UAE students" and references the studio's participation in UAE national game-making competitions (Endless Studios, 2024a). This institutional positioning is not incidental. It reflects a deliberate market strategy in which the UAE functions as a primary operational territory for the platform's educational programs.

This strategic alignment has direct consequences for character design. The development of Emirati characters for Endstar is not a purely representational gesture toward cultural diversity. It is a response to the demographic and commercial reality of the platform's primary user base. For a platform whose educational programs are actively deployed with UAE students, Emirati character representation is a functional requirement of cultural relevance, not an optional enhancement.

### 3.2 The Demographic Stakes

The UAE context introduces a specific representational pressure documented in the sociological literature. Emiratis constitute approximately twenty percent of the UAE population, with the remaining eighty percent composed of expatriate workers from over one hundred nationalities (Khalaf, 2005). In this context, the presence of an Emirati character in a game platform actively used by UAE students carries a recognition function that goes beyond entertainment: it constitutes an act of cultural acknowledgment within a society where the indigenous population is a demographic minority in its own territory.

For Endless Studios, whose mission explicitly includes reaching learners "wherever they are in their journey" and whose UAE programming engages Emirati students directly, this context makes the design quality of Emirati characters a matter of institutional integrity as much as artistic quality.

### 3.3 Contextual Significance for the Academic Article

The Endstar platform thus occupies a dual position in the academic article. As the visual system, it defines the aesthetic constraints within which Emirati character representation must operate, the system coherence requirement. As the institutional context, it defines the cultural stakes of representation, the authenticity requirement. The research problem emerges from the tension between these two requirements, and the article proposes a methodological framework for resolving that tension through evidence-based design criteria.

## 5. Summary of Key Facts for Citation

The following facts are directly supported by Endless Studios official documentation and suitable for citation in the academic article:

1. Endstar is a real-time collaborative 3D game-making platform developed by Endless Studios, a sister studio of E-Line Media, currently in Alpha state (v0.3.7, December 2024).
2. The platform operates a Unity-based SDK and LUA scripting environment and is used as both an educational tool for aspiring game developers and a professionally published game platform.
3. Endless Studios maintains active programming specifically for UAE students and has participated in UAE national game-making competitions, establishing the UAE as a primary operational market.
4. Character and environment art for Endstar is developed by professional artists at E-Line Media and Endless Studios, including the UAE Female character, which constitutes the primary empirical object of the academic article.
5. The Endstar visual system constitutes an established aesthetic framework, with a defined roster of stylized 3D characters, against which all new character designs, including Emirati characters, must be calibrated for visual coherence.
6. The platform's formal incorporation of Endless Studios into E-Line Media occurred in 2026, consolidating the organizational structure under which the Endstar character development work described in the article was produced.
