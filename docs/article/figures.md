## Figure 2. Traditional Emirati Woman Wearing Burqa Facial Mask

**Identified.** Elderly Emirati woman in black abaya and shayla, wearing the burqa
facial mask, photographed against a woven palm-frond (areesh) wall.

The mask covers the nose, mouth and upper lip. The eyes, cheekbones and forehead
stay fully visible, which is exactly the framing effect Wang et al. (2015) propose
as the mechanism behind the Emirati face recognition advantage. The vertical
metallic strip sits along the nasal bridge and reads as a hard graphic line
dividing the visible face.

### Open item, burqa material description

The photograph shows a burqa in dark cloth with a metallic bronze strip, not the
shiny gold fabric the article and the Manual both describe. Both readings have
basis: the traditional cloth is indigo-dyed and its sheen reads gold, bronze or
near-black depending on the piece, its age, and the light. The current text is
narrower than the photographic evidence supports.

Two options, and this one is the Art Director's call since it affects the sculpt:
either widen the article's description to cover the documented range, or state
explicitly that the gold variant is the one selected for Endstar and say why.
Leaving the text as-is means the article describes a narrower object than its own
figure shows.

### Open item, image rights

The article currently declares "Original source unknown. Reproduced for academic
purposes." An unattributed photograph of an identifiable person is a permissions
risk at submission. Either trace the source, or replace it with an image whose
licence is documented. `[NEEDS EVIDENCE]`

## Figure 8. Endstar Character Roster

Four candidate captures of the Endstar visual system, all showing the established
roster the UAE characters must calibrate against.

**Candidate A, formation lineup.** Two long rows of characters facing each other,
roughly forty figures visible. The strongest single image for the roster figure:
it shows the breadth of the visual system in one frame, and the repetition of the
silhouette language across very different designs is the point the caption makes.
Recommended for Figure 8.

**Candidate B, blacksmith scene.** Two characters at an anvil in an outdoor
terraced environment. Best image in the set for the stylization ceiling argument
in Criterion 4: both faces carry constructed eyelid rims, readable socket depth
and simplified planar cheek structure, at exactly the level the article says the
Emirati characters must match. Useful as a second figure if the Discussion needs
a close-range example, since the lineup reads at distance only.

**Candidate C, group shot on deck.** Crowded composition, characters overlapping.
Weaker for a printed figure. Skip.

**Candidate D, development build capture.** In-engine screenshot with the full
Endstar UI, player name, and the Development Build watermark visible. Shows
fantasy-side roster members in a dungeon interior. Do not use as-is: the UI and
watermark date the image and pull attention off the characters. If this frame is
wanted, recapture it clean.

### Open item, clearance

All four are Endless Studios / E-Line Media property. Publishing them in an
academic article needs studio sign-off. Confirm with Daniel before submission.
